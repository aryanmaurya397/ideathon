How to Avail Queue-Free Healthcare?

Three easy steps to receive doctor consultations at your local primary health centre without standing in crowds.

Quick Registration (त्वरित पंजीकरण)

Issue your token in 1 minute using just your phone number or ABHA ID. If you do not have a smartphone, your village ASHA Didi can generate a slip for you instantly.

Need Assistance? (सहायता चाहिए?)

If you need help registering or tracking your token status, please visit the 'Patient Mitra Desk' at the PHC entrance or reach out to your village's ASHA Worker (आशा कार्यकर्ता).

Elderly citizens (60+), pregnant women (ANC), and differently-abled patients can proceed directly to Priority Counter 01 for expedited care.
