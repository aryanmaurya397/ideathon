The Problem We're Solving

Rural Healthcare is Stuck in Lines.

⏳

2–4 Hours in the Queue

Patients stand under the sun just to hold their place at rural health camps — before even seeing a doctor.

📋

Paper Registers = Lost Follow-ups

ASHA workers track everything on paper. Missed vaccinations, skipped refills, no reminders sent.

📵

Apps Don't Work Here

Low-literacy patients can't navigate complex smartphone apps. Many use basic feature phones only.

🏥

No Dashboard for Staff

Medical officers and ASHA workers have zero visibility into patient counts, follow-ups, or health trends.

📊 Over 5 lakh rural health camps run in India annually — most still using paper queues & manual registers

Our Solution

How Swasthya Queue Works

From standing in line → to feeling looked after. One seamless flow for the entire patient visit.

01
🗓️

Register the Patient

Name, age, village, mobile number, and health concern — captured in under 1 minute on any phone or tablet.

Under 1 minute
02
🎫

Get a Digital Token

Patients see their token number and live queue position — no more standing in the sun to hold a place.

Live queue view
03
✅

Consult & Record

Health worker logs visit notes, medicines given, and next follow-up date against the patient token.

Saved to history
04
📱

Auto Reminders Go Out

SMS + WhatsApp messages for follow-ups, vaccinations, and medicine schedules — sent automatically.

SMS + WhatsApp
